## ✅ What Your Crawler CAN Do (After Roadmap)

1. **Niche, targeted data** — Apollo won't have every small VC firm. Your crawler can be better for specific regions/sectors.
2. **Real-time fresh data** — Apollo's data can be 30-90 days old. Yours can be today's.
3. **Custom extraction** — Add your own fields (thesis, portfolio companies).
4. **100-1000 quality records per crawl** — With proper AI filtering, junk = zero.

## ❌ What Your Crawler CANNOT Do

1. **275M contacts** — Requires 2M+ contributors. Network effect.
2. **86-98% phone accuracy** — Phone numbers are not public. They come from email signatures, CRM data, partnerships.
3. **Intent signals** — "This company is buying now" requires third-party data (Bombora).
4. **Real-time verification** — Requires SMTP verification infrastructure.
5. **Enterprise compliance** — GDPR, CCPA, SOC2 — legal team work.
